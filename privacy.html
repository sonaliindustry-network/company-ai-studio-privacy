Privacy Policy for Company AI Studio

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Company AI Studio is an application that helps users create, schedule, and publish social media content using AI-assisted tools.

1. Information We Use

Depending on the features used, Company AI Studio may process your application login information, generated post content, scheduled post information, and LinkedIn authorization information required to publish content on your behalf.

2. LinkedIn Account Information

When you connect LinkedIn, the application uses LinkedIn's authorization system. We do not ask for or store your LinkedIn password. LinkedIn access is granted through OAuth authorization.

3. How LinkedIn Data Is Used

LinkedIn authorization is used only for features you request, such as identifying the authorized account and creating or publishing posts on your behalf. We do not sell LinkedIn data.

4. AI-Generated Content

Post topics and related content may be processed by the configured AI model to generate draft social media posts. In the local development version, Ollama may run locally on the user's computer.

5. Stored Information

The application may store generated posts, scheduled post times, publication status, and related application records in its local database. LinkedIn authorization credentials should be stored securely and should not be shared with other users.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared with LinkedIn only when necessary to provide LinkedIn features requested by the user. AI processing is performed by the AI provider configured by the application.

7. Security

Users should keep application secrets, OAuth credentials, and access tokens private. The application should not be configured with credentials committed to public source-code repositories.

8. Revoking LinkedIn Access

You can revoke the application's LinkedIn access through LinkedIn's account and application settings. After access is revoked, the application will no longer be able to publish through that authorization.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

10. Contact

For privacy questions regarding Company AI Studio, contact the application owner through the contact information provided with the application.
